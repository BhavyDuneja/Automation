# Content calendar

Plan: `reports/campaigns/2026-10-dedicated-experts.md` (campaign "The One-Line Invoice", Mon 2026-10-12 to Sun 2026-11-08). All dates and times are IST (Asia/Kolkata).

**Status values:** `planned` (topic set, not yet drafted) → `drafted` (file exists in `content/queue/` or `content/blog/`, `status: draft`) → `approved` → `posted` / `published`. `open` = a deliberately unplanned slot (about 20%) kept for reactive content or a repeat of the best performer; set it to `planned` with a topic to use it. The unattended daily run drafts only `planned` rows, but it may still propose a gap-based draft for an empty slot: treat that as a suggestion.

**Platforms:** `LinkedIn` posts go out from the founder's personal profile (first person), Tue/Wed/Thu/Sat 09:30. `Blog` and `Page` are answer-first web pieces (one per week). `Email` is a milestone: the copy already exists in `email/sequences/lead-nurture/` and must not be redrafted here. Instagram was connected on 2026-10-11 (`enabled: true` in `context/channels.md`, Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun 19:30). It has no planned rows yet: the daily run fills its slots from gaps and pillars, and every Instagram post needs an image before approval.

**Link rule:** LinkedIn links go in the first comment, with `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<post-slug>`. Tentative slugs for weeks 2-4 are in section 5 of the campaign brief.
