Can you see what your expert is actually paid?

Before you hire through anyone, whether it is an agency, a platform or us, ask to see the invoice line by line.

What I would want to see:
1. The expert's actual salary.
2. The provider's fee.
3. Nothing else.

Here is why I care. An agency may bill ₹1.5 lakh a month for an expert it pays ₹1 lakh. That is an illustration, not a market statistic. I am not saying every provider does this. I am saying you should be able to see it and decide for yourself.

At AnantaSutra the model is simple. You pay the dedicated expert's actual salary plus one transparent fee. No recruitment fee, no lock-in, and you can scale up or pause anytime.

Yes, we charge a fee. The point is that you can see it.

If you are comparing providers this month, ask each one for those three lines. How they answer will tell you a lot.
