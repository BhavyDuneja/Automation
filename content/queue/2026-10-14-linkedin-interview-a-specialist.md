How do you interview a specialist when you are not one?

A clinic owner hiring a growth marketer. A founder hiring an AI engineer. A firm hiring an immigration case manager. You cannot judge the craft yourself, so what do you check?

Here is what I would do:

1. Ask for one result they owned from start to finish, then ask what they would do differently. People who did the work tend to explain the trade-offs. People who watched from the sidelines often cannot.

2. Give them a real problem from your business and ask for their first 30 days. Notice what they ask you before they propose anything.

3. Ask what would make them say no to this job. Honest answers show judgement.

4. Ask them to explain their approach so that you can follow it. If you cannot, that is a risk either way.

5. Interview more than one person. Differences show up fast when two people answer the same question.

The last one is why we introduce 2-3 hand-picked experts instead of one. You interview them and you choose.
