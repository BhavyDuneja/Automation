"Sutra" means thread. It also means a rule short enough to remember.

That is where our name comes from. AnantaSutra (अनन्तसूत्र) means "the endless thread": ananta is endless, sutra is thread.

I think every business needs a few sutras of its own. These are the three we run on:

1. Show the salary. Show the fee.
You pay the dedicated expert's actual salary plus one transparent fee, and you can see both.

2. You choose.
We hand-pick and vet 2-3 experts. You interview them and decide.

3. You can stop.
No lock-in. Scale up or pause anytime.

None of these is clever. They are just easy to check, and that is the point. A rule you cannot check is a slogan.

I split my time between Delhi and Osaka, and we work with clients across 4 continents. Across time zones, a short rule is easier to keep than a long policy, and easier for a new team member to follow on their first day.

What is one rule your team hires by?
