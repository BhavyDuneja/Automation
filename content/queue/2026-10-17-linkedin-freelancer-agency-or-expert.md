Freelancer, agency or dedicated expert? Here is how I would choose.

I run a business built around one of these three, so let me be fair to the other two.

A freelancer fits a short, well-defined task. You brief it, they deliver, you move on.

An agency fits a complex project that needs project management. You are buying a result and a process, and you do not want to manage anyone.

A dedicated expert fits ongoing work where continuity matters. The person learns your business, works on your priorities and sits inside your team. That is what we do: you pay their actual salary plus one transparent fee, with no lock-in.

My rule of thumb: if the work will be finished in a few weeks, hire a freelancer or an agency. If it will run for months and the person needs to know your business, a dedicated expert is usually the better fit.

Not sure which one you need? Bring it to a free consultation. I would rather tell you a freelancer fits than place the wrong person.
