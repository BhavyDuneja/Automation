# Email 1 of 5: what happens next (Day 0)

Subject option A is the default (clear and benefit-led). B is personal and C uses their name; they exist for the A/B test in overview.md.

## Body (copy from here)

Hi {{first_name}},

Thanks for reaching out to AnantaSutra. I'm Bhavya, the founder, and here is what happens next.

1. A free 30-minute call. We talk through the role, the work, and what good looks like for your team.
2. We hand-pick and vet 2-3 experts for you. You interview them and choose.
3. Your expert joins your team (Slack, WhatsApp, stand-ups) and is live in about 7 days.
4. After that come weekly reviews, monthly reports, and one person accountable for how it is going.

You pay the expert's actual salary plus one transparent fee. There is no recruitment fee and no lock-in, and you can scale up or pause anytime.

The only step I need from you is the first one:

Confirm your free 30-minute consultation: https://anantasutra.com/contact

If you would rather tell me about the role first, reply with a line or two and a couple of times that suit you. Replies come straight to me.

Bhavya Duneja
Founder, AnantaSutra

## Notes for the founder (do not paste)

- Timing: fires automatically when the contact joins the "Lead nurture - contact form" group. The 1-hour target holds only if the form submission reaches your email tool automatically; if you add leads by hand, do it within working hours (setup-checklist.md, step 4).
- Personalise: `{{first_name}}` with the fallback "there". At low volume, add one line at the top about what they wrote in the form (the role or the problem). It is the highest-value edit in the whole sequence.
- Exit: if they book, reply or become a client, stop the sequence and handle them personally.
- Check before sending: what does a lead see at /contact? It must let them choose or request a time. If it is only the enquiry form, add a booking link to that page, or change the CTA line to "Reply with two or three times that suit you" and keep the link as the second option.
- Claims check: the four steps, "live in about 7 days" (the site's own wording, no start-date promise), "actual salary plus one transparent fee" and "no recruitment fee, no lock-in, scale up or pause anytime" all come from context/brand.md. Confirm "no recruitment fee" still holds under the fee structure you choose.
- Deliberately not used: statistics, client names and competitor references (too early in the relationship).
