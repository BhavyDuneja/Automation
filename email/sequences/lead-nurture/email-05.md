# Email 5 of 5: the direct ask (Day 21)

Subject option A is the default (honest closure). B is a permission-based question that invites replies. C is a plain question.

## Body (copy from here)

Hi {{first_name}},

This is the last email in this series. I will keep it short.

You asked about a free consultation. Since then I have sent you how to choose between an agency, a freelancer and a dedicated expert, what it costs and who employs the expert, and one real example.

If you are still weighing it, the next step is a single 30-minute call, with no obligation. If we are a fit, we hand-pick and vet 2-3 experts for you to interview, and your expert can be live in about 7 days.

Book your free consultation: https://anantasutra.com/contact

If the timing is not right, that is fine. Reply with "later" and a month, and I will get back to you then. Or tell me what is holding you back; replies come straight to me.

Whenever you are ready, the thread is easy to pick up again.

Bhavya Duneja
Founder, AnantaSutra

## Notes for the founder (do not paste)

- Timing: Day 21. After this email, anyone with no booking and no reply gets the tag "nurture-complete" and moves to the "Dormant leads" group. No further automated emails come from this sequence.
- "Later" replies: add the lead to your follow-up sheet with the month they gave, and keep the promise to get back to them.
- Wording: "the last email in this series" is accurate and leaves room for a separate re-engagement sequence later. Do not change it to "I will never email you again".
- No fake urgency: there is no deadline, scarcity or discount here, by design (brand voice: honest, not salesy). Only add urgency if it is real.
- Claims check: "live in about 7 days" (the site's own wording), "2-3 hand-picked and vetted experts" and the free 30-minute call are from brand.md. The thread line is the one metaphor allowed for this piece.
- If you decide on a trial offer, add one sentence above the CTA.
