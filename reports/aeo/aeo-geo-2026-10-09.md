# AEO / GEO report: anantasutra.com

Date: 2026-10-09 (IST) · Mode: full · First run, so there is no trend yet · Data: `data/aeo/visibility.csv`

## Summary

- **Crawler access and markup are in decent shape.** All 10 AI crawlers tested are allowed (only `/admin/` and `/api/` are disallowed). A sitemap (174 URLs) and `llms.txt` exist, and all 40 crawled pages return 200 and carry JSON-LD. In today's audits of two competitors (8 pages each), Toptal showed no JSON-LD on any page and Uplers had generic Organization, WebSite, WebPage and BreadcrumbList on 6; both publish an llms.txt. Markup is not what separates them from us.
- **The most important page is badly packaged.** `/services/dedicated-experts` (999 words of good content) carries the generic title and meta description shared by 15 service pages, has no Service or FAQPage markup, and has no Search Console impressions. Five domain pages that llms.txt lists hold only 41-48 words, and every crawled page declares a canonical on the apex host while being served from www.
- **Visibility is zero.** anantasutra.com is cited for 0 of 7 buyer prompts (0 of 63 result links) and does not appear for its own name: a search for "AnantaSutra" returns the Hindu *Anant Sutra* thread (Anant Chaturdashi) and a Buddhist sutra.
- **Who wins instead:** vendor-written blogs, "Toptal alternatives" roundups, directories (Clutch, TechBehemoths) and dev.to. Of the 5 competitors now tracked in `context/competitors.yaml`, only Toptal shows up (3 of 7 prompts).
- **Answers discount self-claims** (6 of 7 searches). For the "without markup" prompt the answer says almost none of the firms are truly markup-free. Competitors publish numbers (per `competitors.yaml` notes: Flexiple $299/person/month plus salary at cost; Uplers contract from $2,500/month), while AnantaSutra publishes no fee figure.
- **First AI-referral signal:** GA4 "AI Assistant" = 7 sessions / 7 users in the 28 days to 2026-10-07 (prior 28 days: 8 / 6), 0 key events. It is a baseline, not a trend.
