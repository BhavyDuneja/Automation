## Quick overview
- **What they do:** India-founded provider of "offshore India teams". Three routes: Contractor, Managed Teams (Flexiple is the employer), GCC (your own India entity). [FX-home]
- **Target customer:** "From Series A startups to Fortune 500 companies" [claim]. Contractor route is for "individual entrepreneurs, lean teams". Mostly foreign buyers building in India [inf].
- **Pricing:**
  - Contractor: "Depends on role and experience. We share rates upfront. No hidden fees." Rates are not on the public page. No lock-in. [FX-contractor]
  - Managed Teams: "$299 per person per month, flat" plus "salary at cost". Commitment "Monthly per employee". [FX-managed]
  - GCC: "Higher setup, lower at scale", 4-6 month setup. [FX-gcc]
- **Speed:** profiles in 48 hours; contractor can start in as little as 72 hours; full-time hires typically 1-2 weeks. [FX-contractor, FX-home]
- **Size (third-party estimates, conflicting):** bootstrapped; about $3-3.8M revenue in 2024; 53-77 staff. [3rd, W14]
- **Recent moves:** homepage now sells "offshore India team" (not a freelancer marketplace); about 125 compare and alternatives pages; its Toptal comparison states it was reviewed 2026-08-13. [FX-home, FX-sitemap, FX-compare]

## Their pitch
- **Tagline:** "Your offshore India team, built in weeks, not months."
- **One-liner:** "We find the talent, handle payroll, manage compliance, and run HR, so you can focus on the work."
- **Top 3 claimed differentiators:**
  1. One partner from hire to scale: "Not just an EOR. Not just a recruiter. Not just a staffing agency."
  2. Rigorous funnel: 100M+ profiles, then about 2,000, about 400, about 25, final 4-5, with proctored assessments.
  3. India compliance and HR depth (PF, PT, ESI, TDS, equipment, monthly check-ins) with "one transparent, predictable fee".

## Strengths (be honest)
- The clearest published fee in the market, and it is almost our model: a flat fee on top of salary at cost.
- Fast: 48-hour profiles, 72-hour contractor start.
- Real compliance and HR depth, which foreign buyers value.
- Named case studies with outcomes (CertifyOS: "6 months later: 12 engineers shipping production code"). [FX-managed]
- Covers "any white-collar role"; a large SEO engine (1,808 salary pages, 1,529 role-by-city pages, comparison pages).

## Weaknesses (factual, from their own pages)
- India-only and written for companies building an offshore team.
- On Managed Teams, Flexiple is the employer ("We employ them. They work for you."). "Salary at cost" is not defined on the page.
- No replacement wording on the Managed Teams page (snapshot text, 2026-10-09). Contractor page: "Need a replacement? We'll find one", with no timeline.
- Contractor rates are not public.
- Case studies and logos are tech companies; no clinic, property, legal or immigration proof.
- The message is HR plumbing and compliance, not the expert's business result.
- We found no client-side review data (only Glassdoor employee pages), so complaint themes are unknown. [W7]

## Landmines to set (ask early)
- "Can you see the expert's actual salary and the full monthly invoice, line by line?"
- "If the person is not the right fit in month two, how fast is the replacement, in writing?"
- "Do you need roles outside engineering? Which of their case studies match your sector?"
- "If your needs pause for a month, what happens to the contract?"

## Landmines to defuse
- **"Isn't a flat $299 cheaper than your fee?"** It depends on the salary and what is included. At about ₹92 per $ (rate implied by Bolna's pricing page), $299 is about ₹27,500: 27.5% of a ₹1,00,000 salary, 18.3% of ₹1,50,000, 13.8% of ₹2,00,000 [calc]. Show our number next to it.
