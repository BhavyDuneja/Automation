## Quick overview
- **What they do:** global network of freelance talent (developers, designers, marketing experts, finance and management consultants, product and project managers, sales), plus Toptal Teams, consulting and services, and HireGlobal (EOR, payroll, contractor management). [TP-home, TP-teams, TP-hireglobal]
- **Target customer:** startups through Fortune 500; "Since 2010, 35,000+ clients" [claim]. Experts are "most being located in the Americas and Europe". [TP-faq]
- **Pricing:** no rate card on the pages we read. FAQ: "a flat monthly subscription fee of $79" if you proceed with talent matching. Third-party pages quote $60-$200 per hour (unverified). [TP-faq, 3rd]
- **Speed:** "average time to match is under 24 hours"; if nobody is in network, 1-3 weeks. [TP-home, TP-faq]
- **Trial:** up to two weeks, not billed if unsatisfied; up to three candidates trialled per position. [TP-faq]
- **Reviews:** Clutch 4.8 (cost sub-score 4.5), G2 4.7, Trustpilot 4.8 per a review aggregator; counts differ across sources. [3rd, W10]
- **Recent moves:** Newsweek/Statista 2026 reliability ranking (their claim), a Forbes article (dated 2026-08-05) featured on its homepage, and Teams and HireGlobal expansion. [TP-home]

## Their pitch
- **Tagline:** "Hire the Top 3% of Freelance Talent ®."
- **One-liner:** "The World's Top Talent, On Demand."
- **Top 3 claimed differentiators:**
  1. Elite screening: top 3%, "98% trial-to-hire success rate".
  2. Speed: match in under 24 hours; "Hire in under 48 hours".
  3. No risk: trial period, pay only if satisfied.

## Strengths (be honest)
- The strongest brand and trust signals in the set.
- The broadest domain coverage, including finance, marketing, design and product, at senior level.
- The strongest risk reversal we found in the set (trial, up to three candidates).
- Very fast matching claims and enterprise logos.
- A huge SEO footprint (hundreds of skill and location pages).

## Weaknesses (factual)
- No published rates. Reviewers on Clutch say pricing was higher than competitors, and some want more transparent pricing; the aggregator also notes hourly-billing opacity. [3rd, W10]
- Experts are mostly in the Americas and Europe, so USD pricing and time-zone distance for Indian SMBs.
- Freelancer engagements, not a seat inside the buyer's team; no itemised salary-plus-fee view.
- Premium positioning leaves owner-led SMBs with small budgets underserved [inf].
