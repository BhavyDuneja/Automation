## Quick overview
- **What they do:** India-based "AI-native hiring partner" for engineering, product and growth talent. Three models: Curated Sourcing, Full Service Hiring (pay per hire), Contract Talent (pay per contract). [UP-home]
- **Target customer:** tech companies by stage: early-stage startups, growth-stage startups, scale-ups and GCCs. [UP-home]
- **Pricing:** [UP-pricing, UP-contract]
  - Pay Per Hire: 10%-15% of annual CTC, paid on joining; 90-day replacement; candidate on your payroll.
  - Pay Per Contract: "From $2,500 per month", "Salary and our fee in one rate - nothing charged on top"; talent on Uplers' payroll; lifetime replacement for the engagement; **6-month minimum**; about 2 weeks to deploy.
  - The pricing page is headed "Pricing for India's strongest engineering talent" and shows engineer salary floors of $1,800-$4,000 a month.
- **Scale claims:** 3.5M+ talent network, "Top 1% of India's talent network", 1M+ LinkedIn followers, Google 4.1 (396), Clutch 4.9 (126) on their homepage [claim]; Clutch page today shows 4.8 from 127 reviews. [UP-clutch]
- **Content:** 771 sitemap URLs: 501 blog posts, 104 articles (20 founder stories), 29 case studies; 15 alternatives and 57 "vs" slugs. [UP-sitemap]
- **Recent moves:** "AI-native" repositioning, AI and forward-deployed-engineer role pages, a 2026 article series on India hiring costs (latest read: 2026-06-12), and listings in Multiplier's talent marketplace and Oyster's partner directory (URLs seen in search results; pages not opened). [UP-home, UP-article, W6]

## Their pitch
- **Tagline:** "Hire Top Talents Quickly with AI & Human Intelligence."
- **One-liner:** "Find, hire & manage your team from India seamlessly."
- **Top 3 claimed differentiators:**
  1. Speed: "Get 3 to 5 highly relevant candidates in 48 hours."
  2. Quality: "Top 1% of India's talent network", fill rate "70%" vs "Less than 10%" for recruiting agencies [claim].
  3. Risk: "Lifetime free replacement."

## Strengths (be honest)
- Scale, brand recognition (GitLab, Tripadvisor, Zeals logos) and review volume on three platforms.
- A published menu of models and prices, which most rivals do not offer.
- Lifetime replacement on contracts is a strong guarantee.
- "No entity needed": Uplers employs the talent in India.
- The biggest content engine in the set, including a founder-story series and salary tools.
- Roles go beyond engineering: SEO, performance marketing, growth, content, design and product pages exist.

## Weaknesses (factual, from their own pages)
- The contract rate is "salary and our fee in one rate", so the split is not shown.
- **6-month minimum** on contracts; no pausing.
- The 10%-15% pay-per-hire fee is a classic recruiter fee.
- Public pricing covers engineers only; no price is shown for marketing, design or product roles.
- India-only; a comparison page calls this its main limit. [3rd, W1]
- Almost no content for legal, health, real estate, immigration or finance (2 of about 605 blog and article slugs).
- Clutch reviewers mention QA and revision loops, time-zone friction, wanting more check-ins, and "frequent changes in team members managing their accounts". These are mostly 2021-2024 agency-service reviews, not staffing. [UP-clutch]

## Landmines to set (ask early)
- "Can you pause after month two, or is there a minimum term?"
- "What part of the monthly rate is the expert's salary and what part is the fee?"
- "Which of their case studies are in your sector (clinic, property, immigration, legal, finance)?"
- "Who is your single accountable contact, and how often do you review results?"
