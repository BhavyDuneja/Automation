## 0. At a glance

1. **What:** a 4-week organic campaign that makes the Dedicated Experts offer easy to compare and easy to act on: you pay the expert's actual salary plus one transparent fee, and the expert works inside your team.
2. **How:** founder-led LinkedIn (4 slots a week: 13 planned, 3 left open), one answer-first page or blog a week (4), the lead-nurture email sequence that is already drafted, and week-1 site fixes that make the campaign measurable.
3. **Gate:** GA4 key events, canonical and title fixes, one host and a rebuilt sitemap live by Fri 16 Oct; founder decisions D1-D4 (fee, guarantee terms, employment model, "7 days") by Wed 14 Oct and the Awish details (D5) by Fri 16 Oct.

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Name (working):** The One-Line Invoice. It borrows the flagship page's own phrase, "One-line invoice: salary + fee".
- **Summary:** for four weeks the founder shows in public how a dedicated expert is priced and chosen (salary plus one transparent fee, 2-3 experts to interview, no lock-in), backs it with answer-first pages that Google and AI assistants can quote, and sends everyone who asks for a call into a plain-text email sequence.
- **Primary objective (proposal):** between Mon 12 Oct and Sun 8 Nov 2026, receive at least **3 tracked free-consultation requests** (`generate_lead` + `book_consultation` key events, reconciled with the contact inbox). Baseline: 0 tracked leads and about 88 human sessions per 28 days. Floor 1, stretch 5.
- **Secondary objectives (proposals):**
  1. Founder authority: publish the 13 planned LinkedIn posts on schedule and capture a first LinkedIn analytics baseline (nothing has been posted yet).
  2. Findability: the flagship page has its own title and canonical, reaches 10+ Google impressions and 3+ hire-intent queries.
  3. AI search: 1 of the 7 tracked buyer prompts cites anantasutra.com (or the brand prompt names the company); baseline 0 of 7.
  4. Four answer-first pages live, one per week.
  5. Lead-nurture sequence live by Mon 19 Oct.
- **Reality check:** at about 3 human sessions a day, a count of 1 to 5 leads is within the noise. Month 1 proves the tracking, builds the four pages and sets the baseline. Read the lead number as a log of conversations, not a conversion rate. Search is a slower build; early demand has to come from LinkedIn and direct conversations (performance report §5.3).
- **Soft window:** Navratri and Dussehra (October) and Diwali (early November) usually slow B2B replies in India (performance report §10). Confirm the exact dates; the Sat 7 Nov slot is written to move.

### 4.2 Tactics

**A. Content that intercepts their buyers (factual, no disparagement)**
- W1 blog: dedicated expert vs freelancer vs agency (AEO starred prompt 3). Drafted.
- W3 blog: Toptal, Uplers and Flexiple alternatives, an honest comparison. Fact base (from the battlecards, captured 2026-10-09; re-check on the publishing day): Uplers "From $2,500 per month… 6-month minimum" and "Salary and our fee in one rate" and "10% – 15% of annual CTC" (uplers.com/contract-talent, /pricing); Flexiple "$299 per person per month, flat" plus "salary at cost" (flexiple.com/managed-teams) and "End the engagement. No lock-in." (flexiple.com/contractor); Toptal "flat monthly subscription fee of $79", "trial period of up to two weeks", "up to three individuals … per position" and experts "most being located in the Americas and Europe" (toptal.com/faq). Include a "Last reviewed" date and a corrections email. Check `reports/alerts.md` for a watch trigger before publishing.
- LinkedIn: the five questions to ask any hiring partner (Wed 28 Oct), how to read a comparison page (Sat 31 Oct), no lock-in and replacement terms, answered without naming anyone.
- Answers to complaints found in rival reviews (revisions, time zones, check-ins, team changes) are handled as our process facts (weekly reviews, monthly reports, one accountable contact), never as claims about them.

**B. Same rooms**
- LinkedIn engagement routine (founder, 10-15 minutes a day, estimate): 3-5 thoughtful comments a day on posts by founders and hiring leaders in the target segments, adding a fact or a lesson, no pitches, no links. Reply to every comment on our own posts, especially in the first hour. No pods, no automation tools, no mass DMs.
- Hashtags to test (hypotheses): #hiring, #startupindia, #founders, #remotework. Keep to 3 per post.
- Communities and events: check by hand which of the hypotheses in section 2 are real and active; participate as a member, not as an advertiser. Look up the next SaaSBoomi, TiE and Startup Mahakumbh dates (unconfirmed).
- Review and directory sites where rival buyers compare: Clutch, TechBehemoths, GoodFirms, G2, Google Business Profile. Create or claim profiles with one shared descriptor; ask 3 clients for named reviews with written consent (Mon 19 Oct ask, Mon 26 Oct profiles).

**C. SEO and AEO**
- Targets: starred prompts 1-3 in `context/aeo-prompts.md`; "X alternatives" and "dedicated team vs staff augmentation vs freelancer vs agency" (SEO audit keyword #9); cost and "without markup" questions (keywords #11-12); healthcare growth marketing (keyword #4).
- Gaps covered (see `data/intel/gaps.md`, status `planned`): fee, guarantee terms, alternatives pages, flagship FAQ, non-engineering proof, risk-reversal comparison, canonicals, sitemap and host, conversion tracking.
- AEO visibility runs automatically on Wednesdays (daily-run); read the trend in `data/aeo/visibility.csv`.

**D. Paid (₹0, not used).** If budget appears later: LinkedIn Ads by job title, function, seniority, company size and industry to mirror the audiences above, and retargeting of site visitors. LinkedIn cannot target "followers of competitor X"; the closest compliant proxy is job title plus industry plus company size.
